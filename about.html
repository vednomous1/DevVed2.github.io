ABOUT OUR TEAM

TAU Arnis Team is a proud student organization that promotes discipline, confidence, and excellence through the ancient Filipino martial art of Arnis. We train to build skill, character, and unity while representing our university with honor in every practice and competition.

OUR VALUES

We believe in respect, hard work, and teamwork. Every member is encouraged to grow not only as an athlete, but as a leader, teammate, and role model who carries the spirit of Arnis with pride.

Discipline

We train with focus, respect, and commitment in every practice.

Skill

Our athletes develop precision, timing, and confidence through consistent training.

Unity

We compete as one team and grow together through every challenge.
